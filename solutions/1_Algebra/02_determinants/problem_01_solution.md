# Problem 1: Determinant of a 2x2 Matrix

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
For a $2\times2$ matrix, $\det\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}=ad-bc$.

$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}3&-2\\5&4\end{pmatrix}
=3\cdot4-(-2)\cdot5=12+10=22.
$$

$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}1&7\\2&14\end{pmatrix}
=1\cdot14-7\cdot2=14-14=0.
$$

A square matrix is invertible exactly when its determinant is nonzero. Thus the first matrix is invertible and the second is not.

## Verification
The second matrix has proportional rows, $(2,14)=2(1,7)$, which is consistent with its zero determinant.
