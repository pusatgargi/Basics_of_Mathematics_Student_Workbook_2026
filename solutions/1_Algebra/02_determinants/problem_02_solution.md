# Problem 2: Determinant of a 3x3 Matrix

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
For
$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&-1\\0&3&4\\2&1&5\end{pmatrix},$$
Sarrus' rule gives

$$
\det A=(1\cdot3\cdot5+2\cdot4\cdot2+(-1)\cdot0\cdot1)
-( (-1)\cdot3\cdot2+1\cdot4\cdot1+2\cdot0\cdot5)
=31-(-2)=33.
$$

## Verification
Expanding along the first row also gives
$1(15-4)-2(0-8)+(-1)(0-6)=11+16+6=33$.
