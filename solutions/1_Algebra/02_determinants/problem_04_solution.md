# Problem 4: Triangular Matrix

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
The determinant of a triangular matrix is the product of its diagonal entries:

$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}2&4&1\\0&-3&5\\0&0&7\end{pmatrix}
=2(-3)(7)=-42.
$$

For any upper- or lower-triangular square matrix, its determinant is the product of the diagonal entries. Here that product is $-42$.
