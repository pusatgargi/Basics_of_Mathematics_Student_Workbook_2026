# Problem 6: Parameter and Invertibility

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
For $A(t)=\begin{pmatrix}t&1\\2&t\end{pmatrix}$,

$$\det A(t)=t^2-2.$$

The matrix is not invertible precisely when its determinant is zero:
$t^2-2=0$, so $t=\sqrt2$ or $t=-\sqrt2$.

## Verification
At either value, the two rows are proportional and the determinant vanishes.
