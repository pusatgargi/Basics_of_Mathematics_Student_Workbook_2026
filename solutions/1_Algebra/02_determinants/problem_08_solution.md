# Problem 8: Determinant of a Product

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
Using $\det(AB)=\det A\det B$,

$$\det(AB)=3(-5)=-15,\qquad \det(BA)=(-5)3=-15.$$

Since $\det A=3\ne0$, $A^{-1}$ exists, and

$$
\det(A^{-1}B)=\det(A^{-1})\det B
=\frac{1}{\det A}\det B=-\frac53.
$$

## Verification
The first two determinants are equal because scalar multiplication is commutative, even though the matrices $AB$ and $BA$ need not be equal.
