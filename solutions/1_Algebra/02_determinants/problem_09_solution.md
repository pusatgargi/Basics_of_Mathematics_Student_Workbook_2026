# Problem 9: Area and the Determinant

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
Use the vectors as columns:

$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}3&1\\1&4\end{pmatrix}=3\cdot4-1\cdot1=11.
$$

The parallelogram's area is the absolute value, $|11|=11$ square units. Reversing the columns gives determinant $-11$, but the area remains its absolute value, $11$.

## Verification
Swapping the two vectors changes the determinant's sign, not the parallelogram's area.
