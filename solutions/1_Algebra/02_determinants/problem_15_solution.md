# Problem 15: Combining Determinant Properties

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
By multiplicativity of the determinant,

$$
\det(B^{-1}A^TB)
=\det(B^{-1})\det(A^T)\det(B).
$$

Now $\det(B^{-1})=1/\det B=1/3$ and $\det(A^T)=\det A=-2$. Therefore

$$\det(B^{-1}A^TB)=\frac13(-2)(3)=-2.$$

## Verification
The factors $\det(B^{-1})$ and $\det B$ cancel, leaving $\det A$.
