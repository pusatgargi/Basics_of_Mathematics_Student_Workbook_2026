# Problem 1: From a System to a Matrix

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
The coefficient of each unknown in each equation forms a row of $A$. Therefore

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1&1\\1&3&-2\\3&1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
x=\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\\z\end{pmatrix},\qquad
b=\begin{pmatrix}4\\1\\7\end{pmatrix},
$$
and the system is $Ax=b$.

The first row represents $2x-y+z=4$, the second represents $x+3y-2z=1$, and the third represents $3x+y+z=7$.

## Verification
Multiplying $A$ by the vector of unknowns reproduces the three left-hand sides.
