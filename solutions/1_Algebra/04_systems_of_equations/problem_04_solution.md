# Problem 4: Infinitely Many Solutions

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
The second equation is twice the first. Subtract the third equation from the first:
$2y=2$, so $y=1$. The first equation then gives $x+z=1$.

Let the free variable be $z=t$. Then

$$
(x,y,z)=(1-t,\,1,\,t),\qquad t\in\mathbb R.
$$

## Verification
For example, at $t=0$ the point is $(1,1,0)$. It satisfies the equations: $1+1+0=2$, $2+2+0=4$, and $1-1+0=0$.
