# Problem 9: A System from a Sum Condition

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
Subtract twice the first equation from the second:
$(2x+5y)-2(x+y)=32-20$, so $3y=12$ and $y=4$. Then $x=6$.

The coefficient determinant is
$$\det\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\2&5\end{pmatrix}=5-2=3\ne0,$$
so the two lines are not parallel and intersect at exactly one point.

## Verification
$6+4=10$ and $2(6)+5(4)=32$.
