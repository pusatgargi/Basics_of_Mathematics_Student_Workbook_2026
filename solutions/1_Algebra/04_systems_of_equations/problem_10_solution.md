# Problem 10: A Polynomial Through Three Points

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
Substituting $(0,1)$, $(1,3)$, and $(2,9)$ into $p(x)=ax^2+bx+c$ gives

$$c=1,\qquad a+b+c=3,\qquad4a+2b+c=9.$$

Thus $a+b=2$ and $2a+b=4$, so $a=2$, $b=0$, and $c=1$. Therefore
$$p(x)=2x^2+1.$$

## Verification
$p(0)=1$, $p(1)=3$, and $p(2)=9$, as required.
