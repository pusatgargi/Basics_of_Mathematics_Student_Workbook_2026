# Problem 12: Homogeneous System

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
The second equation is twice the first. The remaining independent equations are
$x+2y-z=0$ and $x-y+2z=0$. Subtracting them gives $3y-3z=0$, so $y=z$; then the first equation gives $x=-y$.

Let $y=t$. All solutions are
$$
(x,y,z)=(-t,\,t,\,t),\qquad t\in\mathbb R.
$$

## Verification
Substitution gives $-t+2t-t=0$ and $-t-t+2t=0$; the second original equation is a multiple of the first. Setting $t=0$ shows that the zero vector always solves a homogeneous system.
