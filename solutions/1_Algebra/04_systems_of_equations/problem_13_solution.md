# Problem 13: Does Every Equation Add New Information?

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
The third equation follows from the first two:

$$
(x_1+x_2)-(x_2+x_3)=10-8
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x_1-x_3=2.
$$

Thus it is redundant. Let $x_2=t$. The first two equations give

$$
(x_1,x_2,x_3)=(10-t,\,t,\,8-t),\qquad t\in\mathbb R.
$$

## Verification
For every $t$, $x_1-x_3=(10-t)-(8-t)=2$, so the third equation is satisfied automatically.
