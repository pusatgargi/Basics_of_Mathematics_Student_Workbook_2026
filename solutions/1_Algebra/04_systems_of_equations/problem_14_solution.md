# Problem 14: A Small Change in the Right-Hand Side

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
The first two equations give $x+y=2$ and $2x-y=1$. Adding them yields $3x=3$, hence $x=1$ and $y=1$.

In the first system, $3x=3$ is satisfied, so the system is consistent with the unique solution $(1,1)$. In the second, the first two equations still force $x=1$, but the last equation requires $3x=3.1$, a contradiction; it has no solution.

## Verification
Substitution of $(1,1)$ satisfies all equations in the first system and fails the last equation in the second.
