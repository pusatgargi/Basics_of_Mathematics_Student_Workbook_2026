# Problem 15: Classifying Three Systems

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
1. Adding $x+y=2$ and $x-y=0$ gives $2x=2$, so $x=y=1$. There is one solution.
2. The second equation is twice the first, so there is only one independent equation in two unknowns. There are infinitely many solutions, $x+y=2$.
3. The first equation gives $x+y=2$, while the second gives $x+y=\frac52$. This is a contradiction, so there is no solution.

## Verification
The three outcomes are respectively one solution, infinitely many solutions, and no solution, as shown by elimination.
